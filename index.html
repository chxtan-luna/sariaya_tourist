About Sariaya

Sariaya is a municipality in the province of Quezon, in the Calabarzon region of Luzon, founded in 1599. It is widely known as the “Heritage Town of Quezon” and the “Art Deco Capital of Southern Luzon,” with more than a hundred cultural properties and ancestral houses, many in Art Deco style.

Mount Banahaw flanks the municipality on the north and Tayabas Bay lies to the south, so one town offers mountain trails, a coastline with beach resorts, and a historic town center. Agriculture is its major source of income.

Festival noted by the Sariaya tourism listing: Agawan Festival (May 15); Senakulo every Holy Wednesday. Confirm dates with the local tourism office.

🏛️ Half-Day Heritage Walk

  1. St. Francis of Assisi Parish Church
  2. Gala-Rodriguez Ancestral House (Rizal St.)
  3. Emralino-Rodriguez, Palabrica & Francisco Rodriguez houses (Mabini / Bonifacio St.)
  4. Don Catalino Rodriguez House (Villa Sariaya)
  5. Mendoza, Barradas & Panganiban houses

⛰️🏖️ Mountain & Coast Day

Plan a trail visit toward Mount Banahaw’s northern foothills (arrange via local tourism office), then head south to the Guis-Guis / Bignay beach-resort area. Check each resort’s current rates and hours directly.
